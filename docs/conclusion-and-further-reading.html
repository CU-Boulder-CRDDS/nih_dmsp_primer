Navigating the NIH Data Management and Sharing Policy (DMSP): Tools for CU Researchers

5 Conclusion and Further Reading

The goal of this primer was to provide a concise overview of the NIH’s Data Management and Sharing Policy, and general guidance on resources that will help you to fulfill Policy requirements. However, it is important to emphasize that every project is unique, and if you have specific questions about how to navigate the DMSP in the context of your own work, please contact CRDDS for a consultation. CRDDS staff are available at regularly scheduled consult hours during the academic ear (see the CRDDS Events Page for more information), and by appointment (please email crdds@colorado.edu).

In addition to consultations, CRDDS regularly provides workshops and educational offerings related to data management (including workshops on some of the tools and best practices mentioned above), which you can also find on the CRDDS Events page.
